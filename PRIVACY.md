# QuietShield privacy policy

QuietShield does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data or browsing data.

- All filtering happens locally in your browser using rules bundled with the extension.
- Settings (on/off per site, blocking mode) are saved with `chrome.storage` on your device only.
- QuietShield makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, telemetry or ads.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/arjun-vegeta/QuietShield/issues
